# mochi-prep

1. Find the meeting. If the person doesn't say which, `list_upcoming_meetings` shows their calendar and booked calls for the coming days.
2. Look back. Search `query_mochi_meetings` for the attendees' names and the topic, and read the last two or three related meetings with `get_meetings`.
3. Check the repo if the meeting is about the code: what shipped since the last call, and what's still open.

Give a brief they can read in two minutes:
- Where things stood after the last conversation
- Open questions and promised follow-ups, and whether they look done
- What changed since then
- One or two things worth raising
