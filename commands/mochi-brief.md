# mochi-brief

Ask for the topic: a project, a customer, a person or a question.

Search broadly with `query_mochi_meetings`, fill in with `list_meetings`, and read the matches in date order with `get_meetings`. Check `list_upcoming_meetings` for calls on the same topic.

Write it as a short story: when the topic first came up, how the thinking moved, and where it stands now, naming who said what. Close with what's still unresolved and any upcoming call where it could be settled.
